# Voice brief

Use this workflow before drafting new material or adapting content for a new audience.

Collect:

- audience and their context
- desired reader outcome
- author's point of view and voice traits
- source material and facts that are approved
- format, length, and publication constraints
- prohibited claims, phrases, or calls to action

Summarize these decisions as a brief. Ask focused questions only for details that would materially change the result. Treat unavailable facts as gaps, not assumptions.
