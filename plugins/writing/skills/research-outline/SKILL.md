# Research and outline

Use this workflow to turn a brief and source material into a defensible structure.

1. Separate known facts, informed opinions, and open questions.
2. Research only sources appropriate to the requested accuracy and recency.
3. Record sources for factual claims that need attribution.
4. Identify the central reader value and supporting points.
5. Produce a logical outline with the purpose of each section.
6. Flag gaps, uncertain claims, and source conflicts before drafting.

Do not elevate unverified material into fact. Prefer primary and supplied sources for product, policy, and technical claims.
