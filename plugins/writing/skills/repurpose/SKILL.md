# Repurpose

Use this workflow to create a new asset from approved source content.

1. Identify what the new audience needs and what the new format permits.
2. Preserve supported facts and the author's intended position.
3. Rebuild the structure for the new channel instead of merely shortening the original.
4. Remove claims, context, or calls to action that no longer fit.
5. Flag facts, links, or time-sensitive references that require a new review.

Deliver the adapted piece with a concise note identifying any unresolved verification.

## Example

Input: a published Substack essay to adapt into a LinkedIn post.
Output: a shorter, scannable LinkedIn draft with a new opening hook and a note on any claim that needs re-verification for the new audience.

## Common issues

- Simple shortening — rebuild the structure for the target channel rather than trimming the original piece.
- Stale references — flag time-sensitive facts or links instead of carrying them over unchecked.
