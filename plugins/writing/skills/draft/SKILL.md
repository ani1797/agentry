# Draft

Use this workflow after the audience, purpose, and evidence are clear.

Write a complete draft that:

- fulfills the stated reader outcome
- follows the approved structure and format
- uses concrete language and active verbs
- distinguishes fact from interpretation
- includes only claims supported by the available material

Preserve placeholders for missing information. Do not fabricate quotations, statistics, examples, personal experience, links, or citations.
